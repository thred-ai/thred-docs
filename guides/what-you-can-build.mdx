Build apps and AI assistants that can get answers over the phone.

- **Shopping assistants:** "Where can I buy a Nintendo Switch 2 in Austin today?"
- **Care navigation apps:** "Which dentists in Austin accept Aetna PPO and have an opening this week?"
- **Rental search tools:** "Which two-bedroom apartments in Austin are available next month under $2,500?"
- **Travel assistants:** "Which hotels in Montreal can guarantee connecting rooms this weekend?"
- **Procurement tools:** "Which suppliers in Dallas can deliver 100 sheets of plywood tomorrow?"
