Build apps and AI assistants that can get answers over the phone.

- **Shopping assistants:** "Which stores in Chicago can deliver and install a Bosch 500 Series dishwasher by next Friday, and what is the total cost?"
- **Travel and event planners:** "Which Boston hotels have 25 rooms and a meeting room for 40 people available next weekend, and what are their group rates?"
- **Claims support tools:** "Which restoration companies in Tampa can start drying a flooded retail store tomorrow, and what are their rates?"
- **Procurement tools:** "Which suppliers near Detroit can deliver a Siemens S7-1200 CPU 1214C next week, and at what price?"
- **Maintenance assistants:** "Which auto glass shops in Seattle can replace a 2023 Tesla Model 3 windshield this week for under $900, including camera recalibration?"
