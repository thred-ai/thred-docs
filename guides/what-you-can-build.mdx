title: "What you can build"
---

Use Thred to discover businesses and verify information that changes faster than their websites.

- Find local providers accepting new customers and check appointment availability.
- Compare current inventory, pricing, or service capabilities across relevant businesses.
- Verify opening hours, accessibility details, or eligibility requirements by phone.

Provide a query and a bounded result count. Thred generates the questions and follows up on unclear answers. It collects information; it does not book appointments, purchase products, or change accounts.

Responses include affirmative, negative, partial, and unsuccessful call outcomes. Use the answer in `content`, call status, and `evidence` to decide what to show in your application. A call without a supported answer has null `content`.
