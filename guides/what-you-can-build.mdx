---
title: "What you can build"
---

Use Thred to add search and supported answers to your application.

- Build a research assistant that answers questions and shows supporting sources.
- Help users find resources, records or programs that meet their needs.
- Compare information across sources using consistent criteria.
- Return structured findings for downstream workflows with optional `output` fields.

For example, a search can ask where to access historical maps, what a community program requires, or how to use a public research facility. Describe the information you need in `query`, and use `results` to bound the search work.

Responses can include affirmative, negative and partial answers. Use `content`, status and `evidence` to decide what to show in your application. Results without a supported answer have null `content`.
